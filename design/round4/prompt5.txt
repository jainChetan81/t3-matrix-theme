1. Watcher size and weight. At 1× (26 px) it is a little faint next to the composer border, and the katakana inside the working eye is mush. Make it 30 px, with strokes about 15% heavier, and keep the disc opaque so the card border never shows through it. For working, keep one cycling glyph in the iris but make it a single bold glyph that fills the iris (no surrounding ticks), so it reads at 1×. Idle must be completely still (shut eye, no breath).

2. Bullet time is too long as designed. A reply can stream for minutes, and a fully frozen field for minutes just looks broken. Change it to: while streaming, the near rain layer pauses and the far layer keeps falling. That gives the parallax "time slowed" feel and the screen stays alive. Keep the single ring from the Watcher, but it should fire a small finite number of times when streaming starts (2 or 3 rings), not loop for the whole stream.
