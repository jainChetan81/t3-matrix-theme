the watcher: i picked b, the sentinel eye

it reads without motion. shut, looking at you, glancing at your text, and red are four different drawings, so each state is clear even under reduced motion.
it’s the only concept that can literally watch you. focused means the eye is open and looking straight out.
it holds up at 26px. the glyph core turns to mush at 1×, the lens can’t show where it’s looking, and the rabbit drifts toward the cute mascot you didn’t want.

it lives on the composer form’s ::before. the card’s ::after is already the block caret, so the red error copy goes on the form’s ::after, which bullet time also uses for its ripple. working is the only state that loops, and that loop runs inside its own small svg. done has no hook of its own: when working stops, the idle or focused rule replays its one-shot, and that replay is the settle. board a shows all four concepts in all seven states at 1× and 4×. board b shows the eye in place on the composer. on the canvas, one-shots repeat every 4 s so you can see them; in the theme they play once.

things i found and fixed

your typing selector doesn’t work. chrome rejects :has(… p:not(:has(> br:only-child))) because :has() can’t nest inside :has(). i used :not(:has(…empty line…)) instead, and confirmed in chrome that the watcher switches to typing once the first character removes the <br>.
possessed’s block caret could never show in t3 code. it was keyed to :placeholder-shown, which a contenteditable never matches. i moved it to the empty-line hook. the look is the same, and possessed grows 18 chars.
base64 is no longer possible. with four svgs inlined, base64 would take the theme to 23,799 chars, so the data: uris have to stay raw utf-8.
the final · possessed boards had a preview bug. their far and still rain layers showed horizontal rows on the canvas. the real css was never affected. i regenerated those three boards with the look unchanged.

the round-4 build check board shows the real possessed.css in chrome: every watcher state at 4×, with and without reduced motion (0 animations running under reduced motion), plus the three touch screens.
